$$
X = \left\lbrace \begin{array}{ll} 1 & \text{SUCCESSO} \\ 0 & \text{INSUCCESSO} \end{array} \right.
$$

$$
\begin{array}{c|c||l}
x & P_X(x;p) & \\
\hline
0 & 1 - p & P(X=1) = p \\
1 & p & P(X=0) = 1-p
\end{array}
$$
la distribuzione

$$
E(X) = p
$$

$$
V(X) = p(1-p)
$$

In forma compatta: $P_X(x;p) = p^x(1-p)^{1-x}, \, x = 0, 1$

- Notazione:
$$
X \sim \text{Bernoulli}(p)
$$
